\section{Counterfactual Anytime Readiness Agent}
\label{sec:method}

\method{} factors action scoring through task-reusable evidence:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:factorization}
\text{prefix}\rightarrow\text{evidence time}
\rightarrow\text{requirement satisfaction}\rightarrow\text{utility}.
\end{equation}
This makes timing explicit and supports new combinations of familiar evidence primitives.

\subsection{Reliability-First Memory}

The memory \rfm{} stores records
$e_i=(k_i,\gamma_i,t_i,x_i,q_i,\mathcal{P}_i)$:
an evidence primitive, grounded bindings, time, pose belief, calibrated quality, and provenance such as frames, masks, and depth support.
Primitives cover grounded location, attribute and relation views, region coverage, calibrated absence, and reachability.
A primitive-specific validator must certify a record before it can satisfy a task; raw detections and language-model hypotheses do not count.
All methods share the same memory capacity, validators, frozen perception and controller, and candidate macro-actions.

\subsection{Joint Evidence--Time Model}

For action $a$ with duration $L_a$, let
\begin{equation}
T_k^a=\inf\{s\in\{1,\ldots,L_a\}:k\text{ is certified in }M_{t+s}\},
\end{equation}
with $T_k^a=\infty$ when the action never certifies primitive $k$.
A single view can jointly establish identity, attribute, and relation evidence, so independent marginals misrepresent conjunctions.
\jet{} encodes \rfm{}, the candidate path, pose belief, and action type with a lightweight Transformer and couples discrete-time primitive hazards through a low-rank mixture:
\begin{align}
p_\theta(\mathbf{T}^{a}\mid M_t,x_t,a)
&=\sum_{c=1}^{C}w_c\prod_{k=1}^{K}p_\theta(T_k^a\mid c,M_t,x_t,a),\\
p_\theta(T_k^a=s\mid c,\cdot)
&=\alpha_{kcs}\prod_{r<s}(1-\alpha_{kcr}).
\end{align}
Residual mass represents $\infty$.
The model also predicts prefix pose/reachability summaries and blockage.
Joint particles sampled from this distribution preserve same-view correlations.

\subsection{Requirement Programs}

Each service template compiles once into a typed program $g_z$.
For example,
\begin{align}
g_{\mathrm{nav}(o)}&=\textsc{Location}(o)\wedge\textsc{Reachable}(o),\\
g_{\mathrm{attr}(o,r)}&=\textsc{Identity}(o)\wedge\textsc{AttributeView}(o,r).
\end{align}
Programs for relations and absence additionally require compatible entity observations or calibrated region coverage.
For joint sample $\mathbf{T}^a$ and prefix $s$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:task-readiness}
R_z(M_t,a,s)=\mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{T}^a}
\left[g_z\!\left(M_t\oplus\{k:T_k^a\le s\},\hat x_s^a;B\right)\right].
\end{equation}
The policy never receives realized task $z$; it uses
$\bar R(M_t,a,s)=\mathbb{E}_{z\sim p(z)}R_z(M_t,a,s)$.
Sample-wise execution avoids an independence product and lets held-out compositions reuse learned primitive dynamics.

\subsection{Arrival-Aware Planning}

With arrival density $f_t$, survival $S_t$, and action cost $C(a)$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:q}
Q(M_t,x_t,a)=\mathbb{E}\!\left[
\int_0^{L_a} f_t(s)\bar R(M_t,a,s)\,ds+
S_t(L_a)V(M_{t+L_a},x_{t+L_a})-\lambda C(a)\right].
\end{equation}
The first term values interruption; the second applies only when the action completes first.
No extra constant discount is needed because real time is already represented.
We approximate the integral with prefix bins and joint particles and perform shallow receding-horizon search.
\method{}-Robust optionally takes a worst-case expectation over an $L_1$ ball around the nominal task prior; it is evaluated only as an extension.

\subsection{Counterfactual Prefix Training}

From a cloned training state, every candidate is executed and true low-level prefixes provide evidence hitting times, pose summaries, and frozen-executor utilities.
Unobserved hits are right-censored.
The loss combines censored evidence-time likelihood with reliability calibration, pose/service-distance prediction, utility consistency, and pairwise ranking for matched-endpoint actions:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{hit}}+
\beta_{\mathrm{rel}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rel}}+
\beta_{\mathrm{pose}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{pose}}+
\beta_{\mathrm{util}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{util}}+
\beta_{\mathrm{rank}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rank}}.
\end{equation}
Branching is a simulator-only training privilege.
At test time the agent evaluates the shared candidates with one \jet{} forward pass and never queries unexecuted futures.
Full likelihoods and pseudocode appear in \cref{app:likelihood,app:algorithm}.
