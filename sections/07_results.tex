\paragraph{Proxy mismatch and timing.}
We test H1 instead of assuming it.
If endpoint proxies remain strongly aligned across arrivals and priors, the motivation must be weakened.
Endpoint-only, prefix-uniform, \method{}, and Arrival-Known then isolate timing:
early-heavy hazards should favor early certified evidence, whereas late-heavy hazards may justify larger terminal gains.
Mini-\taskname{} reports exact regret.

\paragraph{Composition, robustness, and failures.}

Stress tests attribute degradation to duration, sensor noise, hazard mismatch, or limited data.
