\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \taskname{}, where a hidden service request may interrupt any macro-action.
\method{} predicts joint first-hitting times of reusable evidence, composes them through requirement programs, and selects actions with an arrival-aware semi-Markov objective.
\bench{} combines exact tests, ProcTHOR houses, HM3D and OpenEQA scans, HSSD ObjectNav, Habitat-GS photorealistic simulation, dynamic HSSD scenes, and indoor/outdoor deployment on three robots, shifting pre-task exploration from terminal coverage to reliable service readiness at any interruption.
